# Business Model & Operating Policies

## Positioning
Private asset recordkeeping and evidence-based portfolio oversight for HNWIs, corporate clients and family offices. The product is a secure service plus professional operations, not simply a self-service tracker.

## Revenue options (hypotheses, not market benchmarks)
Annual subscriptions/retainers by client type, asset count and service level; separate charges for specialist verification, external appraisals, custom reporting and enterprise integrations. Illustrative pricing ideas: private client $1,500–$5,000/year; premium $5,000–$15,000; family office $15,000–$50,000+; enterprise negotiated. Validate willingness to pay, costs and jurisdictional rules before publishing.

## Operational roles
Client services; registrar; independent reviewer; valuation specialist; compliance/legal; security/engineering; account manager. Define SLAs for onboarding, corrections, review, escalation and re-verification.

## Evidence policy
Define acceptable evidence by asset class and country; source authentication method; ownership vs beneficial interest; review scope; expiration; dispute process; appeals; required external professional opinion. Never represent a document upload alone as verified ownership.

## Financial reporting caveats
Gross registered value is not net worth. To show net worth, separately account for liabilities, ownership percentages, look-through structures, duplicates, currency rates and valuation dates. No performance return claims without robust cash-flow methodology.

## Key performance indicators
Active paying clients; assets recorded; verified-evidence coverage; time-to-review; valuation freshness; document expiry backlog; client retention; security incidents; access exceptions; revenue per client; operating cost per asset.

## Go-to-market
Private pilot with a small number of consenting clients and one or two asset classes; validate verification cost and accuracy before geographic expansion. Trust, confidentiality and transparent service limitations are core differentiators.

# Product Positioning & Services

## Target customers
- HNWIs: consolidated view of personally held assets.
- Family offices: household, trust, company and beneficiary-level reporting with delegated access.
- Companies: corporate asset registers and controlled internal reporting.

## Initial services
1. **Client onboarding:** account, profile, identity/business verification workflow, engagement acceptance.
2. **Asset registration:** administrator-created inventory with unique ID, type, legal owner, location/custodian, identifiers and evidence.
3. **Ownership evidence review:** document checklist, reviewer decision, scope, source, date, expiration and caveats.
4. **Valuation recordkeeping:** reported vs externally appraised values, valuation date, source, methodology, confidence and historical snapshots.
5. **Portfolio intelligence:** category and jurisdiction breakdowns, registered gross asset value, trends, concentration and stale-valuation alerts.
6. **Document vault:** encrypted private supporting files with controlled access.
7. **Reports:** dated portfolio statements and asset summaries with disclaimers and verification legends.
8. **Client service:** correction requests, onboarding requests, notifications and secure support.

## Supported asset classes
Real estate (residential/commercial/land); public securities, bonds, funds and cash accounts; private business equity; vehicles, aircraft and yachts; art, jewelry, watches and collectibles; trust/estate interests; other custom classes. For investments record custodian/account metadata, not credentials. For movable assets, location is a last-confirmed location, not live tracking.

## Asset status dimensions
- **Record lifecycle:** draft, submitted, active, archived, disputed.
- **Ownership evidence:** not reviewed, pending, evidence reviewed, insufficient, expired, disputed.
- **Valuation:** client-reported, administrator-recorded, independently appraised, stale, unavailable.
- **Documents:** pending, received, reviewed, rejected, expired.

Never combine these into a single misleading “verified asset” badge. The interface must show what was checked and by whom.

## Non-goals for MVP
Public lookup of individuals' holdings; automatic global ownership discovery; government title certification; asset custody; transaction execution; financial advice; guaranteed market values; live location tracking.
